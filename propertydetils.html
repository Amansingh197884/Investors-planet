ABOUT PROJECT

Architectural Brilliance & Destination Overview

M3M The Line offers a luxurious retail experience with a unique design by DPA Consultants Pvt. Ltd. The linear structure features double-height stores on the ground floor, three levels of anchor stores, and a prime front façade ensuring maximum visibility and high ROI.

Beyond retail, The Line Avenue boasts a sprawling Food Court, multi-cuisine restaurants on the 4th floor, and Alfresco Dining on the 5th floor—creating a seamless blend of shopping, lifestyle, and fine dining.

M3M Group stands for Magnanimity, Manpower, and Moderation. Standing among the top real estate developers in India, M3M is known for transforming the real estate aesthetics of NCR with iconic residential and commercial landmarks.
